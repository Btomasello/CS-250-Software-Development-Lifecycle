# CS-250 Final Project

How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?
It's important and essential to consider who you are creating for. A program for a specific audience can only be made if there is an understanding of that groups wants and needs. So, first things first would be to research your demographic and know how they want the program to function. This can always change as time goes on, and that is why it is important to remain flexible. There's always the possibility that more features will need to be implemented or cut in the final build. User stories give a glimpse into the persepctive of the end user. If there is positive or negative feedback, the development team can use this to further design the product. To summarize, interpreting user needs is only possible if you understand them, so proper research is necessary. End user factors such as user stories are just one way that the audience can connect with the development team and help to create the final image of the program.

How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?
The development process can be a tedious thing to get started, however, I feel more confident in building a plan to get it up and moving. Proper planning is key. Prior to taking on a project, one must consider their audience. It is also important to understand what is being built and when development will begin. I would approach the development of programs by understanding my demographic, assembling a plan, building a team, setting goals, consistently informing all parties, and delivering results to the customer. One agile process I hope to incorporate into my work is having a strong focus on collaboration. This is a great factor to focus on, however, I also hope to implement the practice of consistently delivering testable product to end users during production. I believe that showing testable results will allow users to feel more confident in their choice to support us. With an understanding of the end users and strong team values, delivering testable product will always be a reality.

What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?
Being a good team member in software development means proper planning and working together. Understanding goals and values makes a world of difference. Having a team that is able to plan and cooperate with one another leads to highly efficient results. A team member who is flexible, reliable, and brings a team mentality to the table is the way I would define a "good member".
